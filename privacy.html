Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AI for Humanity Private Limited (“Maya”, “we”, “us” or “our”) collects, uses and shares personal information when you use the Maya apps, our websites and related services (the “Services”). By using the Services, you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

Maya is operated by AI for Humanity Private Limited, a company registered in India. For privacy questions, write to hello@ciaomaya.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details. Your phone number or email address, the name you give us, and an identifier from Apple or Google if you sign in with them.
  • What you tell Maya. Your messages and requests, and Maya’s replies, so you can see and reopen your conversations.
  • Voice. If you use the microphone, your speech is turned into text by your phone’s own speech service, which is provided by Apple or Google under their policies. Maya receives the text. We do not receive or store audio recordings.
  • Things Maya remembers. Preferences you state, such as a clothing size or a diet. You can see them, delete any of them and switch memory off in the app.
  • Your bag and schedules. Items in your bag and any scheduled actions you set up.
  • Connected services. If you connect an account you hold with a provider, such as a food delivery, ride or booking service, we store the access token it gives us, in encrypted form. Maya reads information from that account, such as saved addresses, only to carry out your request.
  • Device details. A push notification token, device type, operating system and app version.
  • Technical and usage data. IP address, error logs, and records of what you searched for, viewed and added to your bag. These records are linked to your account and are used to run and improve the Services.

What we do not collect. We do not collect your card, bank or UPI details. You enter those on the Provider’s own page. The app does not currently ask for your location, contacts or photos. If a later feature, such as booking a ride, needs your location or an address, we will ask you first.

3. How we use information

  • To provide the Services: to understand your request, search, show results and keep your bag
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure
  • To remember the preferences you have asked Maya to keep
  • To send notifications you have allowed
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, fix problems and improve the Services
  • To meet our legal duties

We process your information on the basis of your consent, which you give when you create an account and use the Services, and as otherwise permitted by law.

4. Artificial intelligence

Maya uses AI models run by third-party providers on cloud infrastructure. To answer you, we send your message, relevant parts of your conversation, the preferences Maya remembers and your first name, if you gave one, to the model. We do not use your conversations to train our own AI models. Please do not share sensitive information, such as passwords, card numbers or health details, in a conversation.

5. Who we share information with

  • Service providers that host the Services, run AI models, deliver sign-in codes and deliver notifications for us. These include Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare, Apple and Google.
  • Providers and catalogues. We send what you are searching for, such as “black hoodie in size M under 2,000”, to third-party catalogues and to the shops, restaurants, delivery, ride and booking services Maya works with. When you continue to a provider’s checkout, you leave Maya and deal with the provider directly. What you give the provider is covered by the provider’s privacy policy, not this one.
  • Services you connect. We send the details needed to carry out a request you have approved.
  • Authorities when the law requires it, or to protect rights and safety.
  • A successor if our business is merged, acquired or sold.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show you advertising.

6. Where information is kept

Our servers and database are in India. AI models that process your messages, and some of our other providers, may run in other countries, including the United States. By using the Services, you agree to this transfer.

7. How long we keep information

  • Account, bag, schedules and memory: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Conversations: until you delete them or delete your account.
  • Sign-in codes: a few minutes.
  • Logs and usage records: for a limited period. They are tied to your account by an internal ID. Phone numbers and email addresses are masked in them. They can include the first part of a message you sent and what you searched for.

We may keep information longer where the law requires it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account. Choose “Delete account” in the app menu. This removes your account, sign-in details, bag, schedules, remembered preferences, device tokens and connected service tokens.
  • Delete a conversation from History in the app.
  • See, delete or switch off memory from Memory in the app.
  • Disconnect a connected service from the app menu.
  • Switch off the microphone or notifications in your device settings.
  • Ask us for a copy of your information, to correct it, to erase it, to withdraw your consent, or to name a person who may act for you. Write to hello@ciaomaya.com.

If you withdraw consent or delete your account, we can no longer provide the Services to you.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit and encryption of connected service tokens. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children

The Services are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new “Last updated” date, and tell you in the app if a change is significant.

12. Complaints and contact

For a question or complaint about your personal information, write to us at the address below. We will acknowledge it within 48 hours and aim to resolve it within one month. You may also complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
